## The 3 Cards

### 1. The Bedfordshire Routine (`fa-mug-hot`)
Balancing day-to-day operations management, refereeing two kids at home, and carving out midnight hours to code. AI gave a regular person the key to unlock creative doors that felt permanently locked away.

### 2. No Fear, Full Send (`fa-shield-cat`)
If AI is supposedly going to upend everything, I’d rather die trying to master it and build my dreams than sit back on the sidelines. I want my kids to see that curiosity beats anxiety every single time.

### 3. Gothic & Unapologetic (`fa-skull`)
I gravitate towards the macabre, dark fantasy, and complex feminist narratives. We tackle difficult concepts head-on, turning shadows into storytelling while keeping the community welcoming and sharp.
