House of Kay: embracing the AI revolution

Hi, Kay here! Mum of two humans and two rescue dogs, Operations Manager based in the UK, and spare time vibe coder (are we still saying that?!). I'm now diving head first into creating the creatures and short stories that have been infiltrating my mind for as long as I can remember, and documenting the journey: the tech stack, the pipelines, the wins, the losses, the frustrations and everything in between.

The day to day routine

Balancing a full time day job, trying not to drag up two kids, navigating neurodiversity, renovating a house, and taking in rescue animals, all while carving out late night hours to code, learn AI, and build short films from scratch. AI gave this regular person the key to unlock creative doors that always felt completely out of reach.

No fear, full speed

If AI is supposedly going to upend everything, I'd rather be wiped off the face of this earth trying to learn, utilise and have fun with it than sit back on the sidelines. Or hate on it! I want my kids to see that curiosity beats anxiety every single time.

Dark, moody and unapologetic

I gravitate towards the macabre, dark fantasy, and complex feminist narratives. I want to tackle difficult concepts head on, taking old folklore, fairy tales, nursery rhymes and fables and twisting them into thought provoking short films and dark visual stories.

Character bibles

Aske

Piskie · Happy Face

Pronounced "ASK uh" (Norwegian and Danish for ash). After a fire took her home, her family and broke her wings in childhood, she decided to take back happiness. Armed with an ornate antique shotgun that fires colour powder, she walks into the Office of Untimely Endings for revenge through joy.

Gristle

Bogle · Happy Face

Around 175 years old and left on a butcher's doorstep as a baby, where two fingers on his left hand were taken as payment. Immaculately dressed in his dark green OUE suit, he has never known love, scrubs his skin red raw, and stamps horrific early deaths on baby certificates without even looking.

Art

Young Bogle · Happy Face

Only 35 (young for a bogle) and wearing a hand me down uniform a size too big from his uncle who got him the job. He doesn't belong at the Office of Untimely Endings, feels pangs of guilt he doesn't quite understand, and hesitates over the stamp rack trying to pick gentler endings and older ages.

Office Bogles · Happy Face

Bored, joyless bureaucrats standing behind towering counters in the counting hall. Built around Clerk 1 (the dark slate bogle Aske shoots first), alongside background variants and the four fingered species hand references built using a real hand pose guide.

Morrowveils

Watcher Species · Happy Face

Nosey moth mammal watcher creatures with glossy black button eyes, small branching antlers, and an occult symbol grown into the skull. They perch in the fog wherever drama is about to happen and physically change depending on whether they feed on celebration or tragedy.
